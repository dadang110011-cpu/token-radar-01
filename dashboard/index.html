How the model works

One formula scores every protocol. Nothing is hardcoded by name: the category weight is read from this snapshot, and the same function scores every row.

implied value = TVL × 0.25 × growth × maturity × chains × category

  • Growth — the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −60%…+150%, weighted 0.5. A hot week lifts value; a cold one trims it; neither dominates.
  • Maturity — a hump over the days since DefiLlama began tracking the protocol: 0.55 at birth, 1.00 at about 18 months, falling to 0.75 after five years. A protocol that has run for years with no token is discounted, not rewarded, for its age.
  • Chains — each chain past the first adds 3%, up to 21%. A token launched into many chains reaches more wallets than one launched into one.
  • Category — 0.85 plus up to 0.30 for the share of this snapshot's protocols in the category that already record a token (smoothed toward the snapshot-wide share). Where tokens are the norm, a pre-token member is likelier to follow.

“Pre-token” means the snapshot records no token for the protocol — no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol. That is the record, not proof: a protocol on the list may still have a token its DefiLlama record does not carry.
